1. Game Concepts

100. General

100.1. These rules apply to any game with two or more players.

100.1a A two-player game begins with only two players.

100.1b A multiplayer game begins with more than two players.

8. Multiplayer Rules

800. General

800.4. Multiplayer games can continue after a player has left the game.

800.4a When a player leaves the game, all objects that player owns leave the game.
Example: Alex leaves the game, and the Aura Alex controls leaves with Alex.

Glossary

Abandon
To turn a face-up ongoing scheme card face down. See rule 701.33, "Abandon."

Ability Word
An italicized word with no rules meaning. See rule 207.2c.
